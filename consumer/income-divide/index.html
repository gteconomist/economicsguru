About this data. The Economic Heterogeneity Indicators are built from a 200,000-household consumer panel maintained by Numerator and are published by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York three times a year — in February, May and September — so this page updates on that schedule rather than nightly. The EHIs are not official estimates of the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, its President, the Federal Reserve System, or the FOMC. Cite as: Federal Reserve Bank of New York, Economic Heterogeneity Indicators, newyorkfed.org.
